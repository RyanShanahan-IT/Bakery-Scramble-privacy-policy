Privacy Policy for Bakery Scramble

Last Updated: [31/10/2025]

Welcome to Bakery Scramble! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile game application ("App").

About Bakery Scramble

Bakery Scramble is a fast-paced merge puzzle game where players drag and drop baking ingredients on a grid to create higher-tier items. Players combine items like flour, dough, cookies, cupcakes, and cakes to progress through the game. The game features a 4x4 grid system where players strategically merge matching items to earn points and advance.

Age Rating: Bakery Scramble is suitable for all ages and contains no inappropriate content.

Information We Collect

Bakery Scramble is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data. All game data, including high scores and progress, is stored locally on your device.

Android Permissions

Bakery Scramble requires the following Android permissions:

1. Storage Permission (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
- Purpose: This permission allows the game to save your high scores and game progress locally on your device.
-What We Use It For: Storing game statistics (high scores, best times) in device storage so you don't lose your progress when the app is closed.
- Data Storage: All data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or share this information with anyone.
- Optional: If you deny this permission, the game will still function normally, but your scores may not be saved between sessions.

2. Vibration Permission (VIBRATE)
- Purpose: This permission enables haptic feedback (vibration) when you interact with game elements.
-*What We Use It For: Providing tactile feedback when you drag items, merge items, or perform other game actions to enhance your gaming experience.
- Data Collection: This permission does not collect any personal information. It only controls your device's vibration motor.
- Optional: You can disable haptic feedback in the game settings if you prefer not to use this feature.

Data Security

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, your information never leaves your device. We do not have access to your game progress, scores, or any other data.

Third-Party Services

Bakery Scramble does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data collection services. The game operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

Children's Privacy

Bakery Scramble is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age. All game data remains on the device and is never shared or transmitted.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app or on our website. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.
